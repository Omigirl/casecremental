# Casecremental — Milestone 2a: Look, Layout, Animation, EN/RU, Audio

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Draft for review
- **Builds on:** `2026-10-07-casecremental-design.md` (base spec) and `2026-10-08-casecremental-campaign-and-table-design.md` (campaign amendment).
- **Origin:** brainstorming session of 2026-10-08 — layout mockups, bot style tests, pixel pipeline demos v1–v8, font and audio choices. Prototype tooling lives in `.superpowers/brainstorm/tools/` (not committed).

## 1. Goal

Make the game *feel well made*: good sprites, backgrounds, animation, effects and sound, in English and Russian. Gameplay stays as in Milestone 1 (your bench, reel, upgrades, cases 1–3); 2a re-skins and re-lays it out, and builds the art, animation, text and audio foundations that M2b–M4 add stations onto.

**Done when:**
1. The game renders as a **pixel-art world with HD light and effects** on the new workbench layout (§3), on desktop and phone.
2. Every on-screen object is animated per the animation standard (§6); nothing is ever still.
3. All art comes through the committed **art pipeline** (§4) from ChatGPT-made source images.
4. All text is in **Press Start 2P**, in **English and Russian** (§7–8).
5. Sound effects and stage music are **ElevenLabs-generated**, mixed per §9.
6. Stations that arrive in later runs are visible as **powered-off silhouettes** (e.g. the sleeping bot).

## 2. Rendering architecture

Two layers, both in the Pixi scene:

| Layer | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixel world** | room, table, mat, cases, key, bins, bot, machines, reel cards, coins on the table | drawn at low resolution into a render texture, scaled up by an **integer** factor with nearest-neighbour sampling. Sprites are never scaled or rotated inside the pixel world (2.5D exceptions below). |
| **HD layer** | lamp light, vignette, dust, glows, eye glow, bloom, particles, coin bursts, screen flash/shake, motion trails, big text pops | full resolution over the pixel world; smooth, 60 fps |

- **World size:** height 180 px; scale = largest integer with `180 × scale ≤ viewport height` (×6 at 1080p, ×4 at 720p); width = `ceil(viewport width / scale)` so the world fills the screen without letterboxing. Layout is computed in world pixels.
- **2.5D exceptions (user decision, 2026-10-08):** the **camera zoom** scales the whole scene (pixel view + HD layer) around the reveal on big moments — a punch on Covert, a ~30 % push-in during the ★ slow-mo — and returns to exactly 1; a **dragged item is lifted** into the HD layer, drawn 15 % larger, gliding with the pointer, leaning into the drag with a soft shadow, and drops back into the pixel grid on release.
- **React UI** (side panels, HUD, settings) sits on top as DOM, drawn with pixel-art 9-slice frames (`image-rendering: pixelated`) and Press Start 2P, positioned in world-pixel units × scale so it lines up with the pixel world.
- The existing `sim/`, `game/` and controller stay unchanged; `fx/` is reorganised into `fx/pixel/` and `fx/hd/`.

## 4. Art pipeline

### 4.1 Sources
- All sprites and backgrounds are generated by the user in **ChatGPT** from prompts in `art/PROMPTS.md`, always attaching the style reference **`art/style/bot-reference.png`** (the hi-bit bot) and asking for transparent PNGs.
- Animated sprites are requested as **sheets of 2 rows × 4 frames** with large, detailed frames ("as detailed as the reference").
- Source files go to `art/source/<asset-id>.png` (committed — they are the masters).

### 4.2 Processing (`tools/art/`, Node, no extra dependencies)
Committed, unit-tested scripts that turn a source into game-ready sprites:
1. **Reflow** multi-row sheets into one row, aligned on each row's ground line.
2. **Slice** frames by connected shapes owned by equal columns (handles touching frames).
3. **v2 recipe:** smooth area downscale to the asset's target height (bot: 48 px), **posterize** each channel in steps of 48, hard alpha (≥ 50%), 1 px dark outline `#120e10`.
4. **Align** frames on a common ground line and the centre of the feet.
5. **Eyes (characters only):** erase painted eyes and their blended halo; record one **level** eye row and two eye x positions per frame.
6. Write `src/modules/casecremental/assets/sprites/<asset-id>.png` (frame strip) + `<asset-id>.json` (frame size, ground, eyes, pivots).

`art/manifest.json` lists every asset: id, source file, kind (static / sheet), frames, target height, eyes on/off. `npm run art` rebuilds all sprites.

## 5. Skins in the pixel world

- A skin = a **weapon base sprite** (greyscale, shaded, ~32×12 px) × its **procedural pattern** (existing `fx/skins/patterns.ts`, rendered at pixel resolution) — pattern colours multiplied by the base's shading, clipped to its silhouette, plus the 1 px outline.
- Cached per skin; reel cards, the reveal card and tray items use it.

## 6. Animation standard

**Rule:** nothing on screen is ever still. Every object has up to three motion layers:

| Layer | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Idle | always on, subtle, never in sync | bot breathing, lamp flicker, dust, key glint, bin pulse, coin glint |
| Reaction | to the player's touch | buttons press in and spring back, cases hop when bought, the key wiggles when grabbed, dragged items lean |
| Event | the game happening | reveal (anticipation → hit → follow-through), hit-stop on Covert, slow-mo on ★, coins flying to the counter, stage-up sweep, station power-on |

**Pixel sprites:**
- Motion comes from **drawn frames** plus **whole-pixel in-betweens** (parts or the whole sprite nudged by 1–2 px between drawn frames), as in demo v8.
- **Per-frame timing** (each frame has its own duration); anticipation, holds, squash on landing.
- No scaling or rotation inside the pixel world (the camera zoom and lifted items, §2, are the exceptions). Characters split into head / body / legs bands may bob 1 px out of step; the head band is drawn one row taller to hide seams.
- **Code-drawn eyes** on dark visor patches: 2×3 blocks; states open / half / closed / happy (^ ^); blink every 2–5 s; look toward the action.

**HD layer and UI:** GSAP timelines with shared presets (pop, squash-land, anticipate-then-act, overshoot, spring).

## 7. Typography

- **Press Start 2P** for all text, bundled with the game (npm `@fontsource/press-start-2p`, Latin + Cyrillic) — not loaded from Google Fonts, because the embedded game may only reach platform hosts.
- Sizes are multiples of 8 px. It is monospaced, so a label's width is `characters × size`: every label has a **character budget** (e.g. upgrade name ≤ 14, button ≤ 10).
- Money and pops: gold gradient fill, 2 px dark drop shadow.

## 8. English / Russian

- Language follows the platform/browser language; Settings has an EN / RU toggle (remembered in local storage — a per-player convenience).
- Strings in `i18n/en.ts` and `i18n/ru.ts` with typed keys; content names (cases, weapons, skins, rarities, upgrades, stages) are localized — parody names in both languages (Boot Camp → «Учебка», rarities: Армейское, Запрещённое, Засекреченное, Тайное, ★ Редкий предмет).
- Numbers: K / M / B / T suffixes in both languages; Russian uses a decimal comma (`$12,4K`).
- Tests: every key exists in both languages; every string fits its character budget.

## 9. Audio

- **Feel:** tactile, crisp, satisfying — inspired by Scritchy Scratchy: every action sounds physical; cozy at first, louder and wilder as intensity rises. Not 8/16-bit.
- **Sound effects** generated with the **ElevenLabs MCP**: case latch, key slide + turn, reel ratchet ticks, lid creak, coin clink / cascade, cash register, buttons, panel open, stage-up whoosh, Covert impact, ★ jackpot swell, bot servo whirs + beeps (for M3). 3–5 variants for frequent sounds; random ±4% pitch and ±2 dB per play; coin/tick pitch rises with the combo.
- **Music** generated with ElevenLabs: one loop per intensity stage at a **shared tempo and key**; 2a ships the Sober and Buzzed loops (the rest arrive with their stages). Stage changes crossfade on bar boundaries.
- **Mixer:** music / SFX / UI buses, music ducks under big reveals, a limiter on the master; Settings has music and SFX volume. Audio unlocks on the first user gesture.
- Files: `src/modules/casecremental/assets/audio/` (mp3), listed in `art/manifest.json` with their generation prompts.

## 10. Testing

- Unit tests for `tools/art/` on synthetic images: reflow, frame ownership, posterize, outline, eye erase, alignment.
- i18n tests (§8), layout tests for desktop and phone sizes (world-pixel bounds, panels never overlapping benches).
- Visual check in Chrome per task; screenshots at desktop and phone sizes.

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT sheets inconsistent between frames | 2×4 detailed sheets + shared posterize; re-roll a sheet; splice extra frames |
| Asset count/time | Asset list is fixed per milestone; placeholders allowed until the art lands |
| Font or audio too big for the bundle | Latin + Cyrillic subsets only; mp3 at 96–128 kbps |
| Integer scaling leaves odd space on some screens | World width extends to fill; height fixed at 180 px |
| Hop timing felt off in demo v8 | Re-time the hop (crouch → takeoff → peak hold) when the bot is integrated (M3) |
